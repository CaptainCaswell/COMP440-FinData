# COMP 440 - FinData

## Stage 1 - Data Availability

The purpose of this stage is to work through what data is available and the best way to collect and store the data. The goal is to get relevant data for all stocks in the last 5 years.

## Relevant Data

For this stage, we will be looking for the following data:

* 1D, 5D, 1M, 6M, 1Y, 3Y, 5Y, YTD prices
* Beta
* PS Ratio
* P/E Ratio
* Market Cap
* Eearnings per share
* Average Volume
* 1 year target Mean
